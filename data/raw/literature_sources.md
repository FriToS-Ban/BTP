# Sources for literature_compilation.csv

**Provenance.** This table was compiled during the project's literature search by
transcribing values from the open-access papers listed below. Two sources were
re-checked against the originals by script: S01 (Table 2, exact match) and S09 (exact
match; the values are in Table 4 of the PMC version, which the journal layout numbers
differently). Verify any row against its cited table before quoting it formally.

93 rows from 26 open-access sources. Every number was read from a table, or from the running text where the row's `table_or_section` field says so. Nothing was digitised from figures or estimated.

## Conventions

- **Blank cells** mean the value was not reported. Values given only as "<x" (for example P < 0.002) are left blank and listed in `notes`.
- **Unit conversions** (the only arithmetic applied):
  - strain fractions to % (0.21 to 21)
  - Å or µm to nm for pearlite spacing
  - K to °C for austenitizing temperature (the original K value is kept in `notes` or `condition`)
- **`condition`** uses `annealed_furnace_cooled`, `normalized_air_cooled` or `hot_rolled_air_cooled` only when the paper states that route. Everything else is `other: ...` with the route spelled out (thermo-mechanical routes, isothermal transformation, coiling simulation, cyclic treatments, as-received).
- **`ferrite_fraction_pct`** is filled only when the paper reports it. It is never computed as 100 minus pearlite.

## Retrieval note

J-STAGE served PDFs at about 4 KB/s and cut connections after roughly 2.5 minutes. For S02, S06, S16, S19 and S26 only the first pages of the PDF could be downloaded. Those pages were rebuilt locally and read as page images. Every table transcribed from them was complete and legible. Values on pages that could not be retrieved are absent from the CSV.

## Examined but not used

- **Figures only, or no usable table:**
  - Shirasawa et al., Tetsu-to-Hagané 67 (1981) 2208
  - Mizuno et al., Tetsu-to-Hagané 96 (2010) 414 (also truncated)
  - Materials 14 (2021) 2309 (PMC8125484)
  - Appl. Microsc. 2022 carbon-doped AISI steels (PMC9583967; hardness in figures)
  - ISIJ Int. 55 (2015) ISIJINT-2015-407 (pearlite % only as micrograph labels)
- **Spheroidized, nominal composition only, or not ferrite–pearlite:**
  - 42CrMo4 annealing (PMC7254350)
  - AISI 1040 hardened-and-tempered cups (ISIJ Int. 56 (2016))
  - harmonic powder-metallurgy Fe-0.3C (Tetsu-to-Hagané 106 (2020))
- **Inconsistent data:** RE-treated Nb steel, Materials 17 (2024) 1701. It reports 0.05C with 21.6 % pearlite and UTS 610 MPa, with no heat-treatment details.
- **Access:**
  - NIMS Fatigue Data Sheets prohibit redistribution and bulk extraction.
  - The Korean J. Heat Treat. 2016 hypoeutectoid study could not be downloaded (server throttled).

## Sources added in the second search (S27-S52)

A second, wider search looked for open papers that test the same steel after furnace cooling and after air
cooling, and for alloy steels air cooled as bars. Every number was transcribed from a table or the running
text with a verbatim excerpt (`literature_evidence.csv`), and a script then checked that every number of
every row appears in the saved full text of its source (122 of 122 rows passed). Rows whose values are
implausible or internally inconsistent carry `quality_flag = suspect: <reason>` and are kept for
transparency but left out of every metric. Many of the papers that test both routes come from weak
journals and are flagged; the cleaner sources are mostly single-route.
