Equations as Implemented

Compositions are in wt% and temperatures in °C unless stated, and where we limited a published relation, the change is stated.

Transformation Kinetics

The rates of the Kirkaldy–Venugopalan model as modified by Li et al. , with the grain size number G, the gas constant R = 1.987 cal mol−1 K−1 and Q = 27 500 cal mol−1, are

k_F = f_F\, \frac{2^{0.41 G}\, (\mathrm{Ae}_3 - T)^3\, e^{-Q/RT}}{\mathrm{FC}}, \qquad k_P = f_P\, \frac{2^{0.32 G}\, (\mathrm{Ae}_1 - T)^3\, e^{-Q/RT}}{\mathrm{PC}} \\ k_B = f_B\, \frac{2^{0.29 G}\, (B_s - T)^2\, e^{-Q/RT}}{\mathrm{BC}} \mathrm{FC} = \exp(1.00 + 6.31\,\mathrm{C} + 1.78\,\mathrm{Mn} + 0.31\,\mathrm{Si} \\ + 1.12\,\mathrm{Ni} + 2.70\,\mathrm{Cr} + 4.06\,\mathrm{Mo}) \mathrm{PC} = \exp(-4.25 + 4.12\,\mathrm{C} + 4.36\,\mathrm{Mn} + 0.44\,\mathrm{Si} \\ + 1.71\,\mathrm{Ni} + 3.33\,\mathrm{Cr} + 5.19\sqrt{\mathrm{Mo}}) \mathrm{BC} = \exp(-10.23 + 10.18\,\mathrm{C} + 0.85\,\mathrm{Mn} \\ + 0.55\,\mathrm{Ni} + 0.90\,\mathrm{Cr} + 0.36\,\mathrm{Mo})

with fF = fP = 1 as published and fB = 79.4 with ΔBs = −107 °C from the calibration on the hardenability bands. Ferrite growth is limited by the extrapolated Ae3 lever rule, and the prior-austenite grain size after about 1 h at T is

G = 8 - \frac{\max(T - 850,\ 0)}{100}, \qquad 4 \le G \le 9

Cementite Solvus

The cementite solvus used for the Hultgren condition is the fit used by Collins et al. ,

\mathrm{Ae}_{cm} = 224.4 + 992.4\,\mathrm{C} - 465.1\,\mathrm{C}^2 + 46.7\,\mathrm{Cr} + 19.0\,\mathrm{C}\,\mathrm{Cr} - 6.1\,\mathrm{Cr}^2 \\ + 7.6\,\mathrm{Mn} + 10.0\,\mathrm{Mo} - 6.8\,\mathrm{Cr}\,\mathrm{Mo} - 6.9\,\mathrm{Ni} + 3.7\,\mathrm{C}\,\mathrm{Ni} \\ - 2.7\,\mathrm{Cr}\,\mathrm{Ni} + 0.8\,\mathrm{Ni}^2 + 16.7\,\mathrm{Si}

and above about 0.9% C the upper critical temperature for the default normalising temperature is drawn from the eutectoid point with the slope of the binary Fe–Fe3C solvus, about 304 °C per % C.

Hardness of Bainite and Ferrite–Pearlite

The relations of Maynier et al. , with Vr the cooling rate at 700 °C in °C/h, are

\mathrm{HV}_B = -323 + 185\,\mathrm{C} + 330\,\mathrm{Si} + 153\,\mathrm{Mn} + 65\,\mathrm{Ni} + 144\,\mathrm{Cr} + 191\,\mathrm{Mo} \\ + (89 + 53\,\mathrm{C} - 55\,\mathrm{Si} - 22\,\mathrm{Mn} - 10\,\mathrm{Ni} - 20\,\mathrm{Cr} - 33\,\mathrm{Mo}) \log V_r \mathrm{HV}_{FP} = 42 + 223\,\mathrm{C} + 53\,\mathrm{Si} + 30\,\mathrm{Mn} + 12.6\,\mathrm{Ni} + 7\,\mathrm{Cr} + 19\,\mathrm{Mo} \\ + (10 - 19\,\mathrm{Si} + 4\,\mathrm{Ni} + 8\,\mathrm{Cr} + 130\,\mathrm{V}) \log V_r

Brinell hardness is taken as 0.95 HV below about 450 HV, and Rockwell C hardness follows the conversion table of ASTM E140 .

Ferrite Grain Size and Elongation

The ferrite grain size follows Hodgson and Gibbs with the carbon equivalent Ceq = C + Mn/6 held at 0.35 at most,

d_\alpha = (-0.4 + 6.37\,C_{eq}) + (24.2 - 59.0\,C_{eq})\, \dot{T}^{-1/2} \\ + 22\left[1 - \exp(-0.015\, d_\gamma)\right]\ \ \mu\mathrm{m}

for cooling rates Ṫ of at least 0.1 K/s, clipped to 3 to 60 µm. Elongations measured on different gauges are converted with Oliver's relation ,

A_2 = A_1 \left( \frac{k_1}{k_2} \right)^{0.4}, \qquad k = \frac{L_0}{\sqrt{S_0}}

and the uniform elongation eu = 0.508 times the total elongation on the 4.5√S0 gauge, with n = ln(1 + eu) as the Considère uniform true strain.

Precipitation

The precipitation strengthening is added only above 0.03% V or 0.01% Nb, and the solubility products, in wt% with T in kelvin , are

\log[\mathrm{V}][\mathrm{C}] = 6.72 - \frac{9500}{T}, \qquad \log[\mathrm{V}][\mathrm{N}] = 3.63 - \frac{8700}{T} \\ \log[\mathrm{Nb}]\left[\mathrm{C} + \tfrac{12}{14}\mathrm{N}\right] = 2.26 - \frac{6770}{T}
